# 5/12 화

I hung out with some friends yesterday. After work, the three of us **met up at a café**, and we’re all developers. These days, we keep circling the same question: **how are we supposed to live now that AI is here?**

One of my friends said they’ve decided to focus on writing. They used to dream about becoming a **good developer**, but they told us they’d **lost confidence in themselves**—not just because they were having a bad week, but **on a deeper level**. They don’t feel like they can count on effort alone to **make it through what’s coming**. They also said that if they died tomorrow, they’d probably regret never giving their childhood dream of becoming a writer a **real chance**. So lately, they’ve been reading a lot and writing.

I’ve always enjoyed reading and writing too, but I don’t feel that **desperate need to be great at it**. I’ve heard people say that some artists feel like they’d die if they couldn’t create, and I get the romantic version of that, but it’s not really me. If someone offered me a choice—**ten billion won or Han Kang–level talent as a writer**—I’d take the money and **call it a day**. If I’d had that kind of **all-consuming passion** from the beginning, I probably wouldn’t be living the way I am now.

Still, when I think about **the ten years I poured into exam prep**—years that sometimes feel like a waste—I can’t help wondering: **what would’ve happened if I’d trusted myself enough to invest that time in what I actually wanted to get good at?** That regret is real. But if I don’t want to feel the same way ten years from now, **it’s not too late to start**.
