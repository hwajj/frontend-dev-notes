# Lesson 009: Be Verb Questions

## Be동사 의문문 기본 구조

<details>
<summary>표현</summary>

### 현재

- **Are you ~?** — ~해? / ~야?
- **Is he/she ~?** — 그 사람은 ~해? / ~야?
- **Are they ~?** — 그들은 ~해? / ~야?

### 과거

- **Were you ~?** — ~했어? / ~였어?
- **Was he/she ~?** — 그 사람은 ~했어? / ~였어?
- **Were they ~?** — 그들은 ~했어? / ~였어?

### 미래

- **Will you be ~?** — ~할 거야? / ~일 거야?
- **Will he/she be ~?** — 그 사람은 ~할 거야? / ~일 거야?
- **Will they be ~?** — 그들은 ~할 거야? / ~일 거야?

</details>

---

## Are → Were → Will be

<details>
<summary>예문</summary>

### 현재

**Are you busy?**
바빠?

**Are you tired?**
피곤해?

**Are you interested in English?**
영어에 관심이 있어?

### 과거

**Were you busy yesterday?**
어제 바빴어?

**Were you tired after work?**
퇴근하고 피곤했어?

**Were you interested in English when you were younger?**
어렸을 때 영어에 관심이 있었어?

### 미래

**Will you be busy tomorrow?**
내일 바쁠 거야?

**Will you be tired after work tomorrow?**
내일 퇴근하면 피곤할 것 같아?

**Will you still be interested in English in the future?**
앞으로도 계속 영어에 관심이 있을 것 같아?

</details>

## Be동사 + What

<details>
<summary>표현</summary>

- **What are you ~?** — 뭐가 ~해? / 무엇에 ~해?
- **What were you ~?** — 뭐가 ~했어? / 무엇에 ~했어?
- **What will you be ~?** — 앞으로 어떤 ~일 거야?

</details>

<details>
<summary>예문</summary>

앞으로는 뭘 배우는 데 관심이 생길 것 같아?

뭘 잘해?

어렸을 때 뭘 잘했어?

**What will you be good at in the future?**
앞으로는 뭘 잘하게 될 것 같아?

---

**What are you worried about?**
뭐가 걱정돼?

**What will you be worried about at your next job?**
다음 직장에서는 뭐가 걱정될 것 같아?

## Be동사 + How

<details>
<summary>표현</summary>

- **How are you?** — 어떻게 지내?
- **How were you feeling?** — 기분이 어땠어? / 상태가 어땠어?
- **How long are you going to be ~?** — 얼마나 오래 ~할 거야?
- **How long were you ~?** — 얼마나 오래 ~했어?
- **How long will you be ~?** — 얼마나 오래 ~할 거야?

</details>

<details>
<summary>예문</summary>

**How are you these days?**
요즘 어떻게 지내?

**How were you feeling when you started your first job?**
첫 직장을 시작했을 때 기분이 어땠어?

**How will you feel when you become a better developer?**
더 실력 있는 개발자가 되면 기분이 어떨 것 같아?

---

**How long are you going to be there?**
거기 얼마나 오래 있을 거야?

**How long were you unemployed?**
실업 상태로 얼마나 오래 있었어?

**How long will you be at your current company?**
현재 회사에서 얼마나 오래 일할 것 같아?

</details>

## Be동사 + 상태 / 감정

<details>
<summary>표현</summary>

- **Are you happy with ~?** — ~에 만족해?
- **Are you worried about ~?** — ~이 걱정돼?
- **Are you afraid of ~?** — ~이 두려워?
- **Are you interested in ~?** — ~에 관심이 있어?
- **Are you good at ~?** — ~을 잘해?
- **Are you comfortable with ~?** — ~이 편해? / ~하는 게 편해?

</details>

<details>
<summary>예문</summary>

**Are you happy with your current job?**
지금 직장에 만족해?

**Were you happy with your first job?**
첫 직장에 만족했어?

**Will you be happy with your next job?**
다음 직장에서는 만족할 것 같아?

---

**Are you worried about making the wrong decision?**
잘못된 결정을 내릴까 봐 걱정돼?

**Were you worried about making decisions when you were younger?**
어렸을 때 결정을 내리는 게 걱정됐어?

**Will you be less worried about making decisions in the future?**
앞으로는 결정을 내릴 때 걱정이 좀 줄어들 것 같아?

---

**Are you comfortable with your own choices?**
자신이 한 선택에 만족해?

**Were you comfortable making your own choices when you were younger?**
어렸을 때 스스로 선택하는 게 편했어?

**Will you be more comfortable making your own decisions in the future?**
앞으로는 스스로 결정하는 게 더 편해질 것 같아?

</details>

## 핵심 패턴 정리

<details>
<summary>정리</summary>

### Be동사 의문문

**Are you + 상태/성격/감정?**
→ 현재

**Were you + 상태/성격/감정?**
→ 과거

**Will you be + 상태/성격/감정?**
→ 미래

### 의문사 추가

**의문사 + be동사 + 주어 ~?**

- **What are you ~?**
- **Why are you ~?**
- **Where are you ~?**
- **When are you ~?**
- **How are you ~?**

과거라면:

- **What were you ~?**
- **Why were you ~?**
- **Where were you ~?**
- **When were you ~?**
- **How were you ~?**

미래라면:

- **What will you be ~?**
- **Why will you be ~?**
- **Where will you be ~?**
- **When will you be ~?**
- **How will you be ~?**

</details>
