# Lesson 002: Negative Sentences

## Work

<details>
<summary>표현</summary>

### 현재 부정

- **don't work on Fridays** — 금요일에는 일하지 않다
- **don't work from home** — 재택근무를 하지 않다
- **don't like my job** — 내 직업을 좋아하지 않다

### 과거 부정

- **didn't quit my job** — 직장을 그만두지 않았다
- **didn't get along with ~** — ~와 잘 지내지 못했다
- **wasn't interested in ~** — ~에 관심이 없었다

</details>

<details>
<summary>예문</summary>

I don't work on Fridays.

I don't work from home.

I don't really like my current job.

I didn't quit my job because I hated it.

I wasn't interested in front-end development at first.

I didn't get along with some of my coworkers.

</details>

## Study

<details>
<summary>표현</summary>

### 현재 부정

- **don't have time to ~** — ~할 시간이 없다
- **don't feel like ~ing** — ~할 기분이 아니다
- **don't study every day** — 매일 공부하지 않는다

### can't

- **can't concentrate on ~** — ~에 집중할 수 없다

### 과거 부정

- **didn't study yesterday** — 어제 공부하지 않았다

</details>

<details>
<summary>예문</summary>

I don't have much time to study after work.

I don't feel like studying when I'm tired.

I don't study every day.

I can't concentrate on studying at night.

I didn't study yesterday because I was too tired.

</details>

## Workout

<details>
<summary>표현</summary>

### 현재 부정

- **don't work out every day** — 매일 운동하지 않다
- **don't go to the gym** — 헬스장에 가지 않다
- **don't stay up late** — 늦게까지 깨어 있지 않다

### can't

- **can't go to the morning class** — 아침 수업에 갈 수 없다

### 과거 부정

- **didn't work out yesterday** — 어제 운동하지 않았다

</details>

<details>
<summary>예문</summary>

I don't work out every day.

I don't go to the gym. I do CrossFit instead.

I don't usually stay up late.

If I stay up past 1 a.m., I can't go to the morning class.

I didn't work out yesterday because I was too tired.

</details>

## Cooking

<details>
<summary>표현</summary>

### 현재 부정

- **don't cook every day** — 매일 요리하지 않다
- **don't enjoy ~ing** — ~하는 것을 즐기지 않다
- **don't like ~** — ~을 좋아하지 않다
- **don't make fancy dishes** — 근사한 요리를 만들지 않다

### can't

- **can't make ~** — ~을 만들 수 없다

</details>

<details>
<summary>예문</summary>

I don't cook every day.

I don't enjoy making a lot of side dishes.

I don't like making fancy dishes.

I can't make complicated dishes very well.

I don't usually make fancy meals. I prefer simple one-dish meals.

</details>
