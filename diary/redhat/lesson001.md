# Lesson 001: Negative Sentences

## Work

<details>
<summary>표현</summary>

### 일하는 시간·기간

- **work 34 hours a week** — 일주일에 34시간 일하다
- **work seven hours a day** — 하루에 7시간 일하다
- **from Monday to Thursday** — 월요일부터 목요일까지
- **on Fridays** — 금요일에는
- **for a year and a half** — 1년 반 동안
- **for over ten years** — 10년 넘게
- **when I was 31** — 내가 31살이었을 때

### 취업·회사

- **start working in 2021** — 2021년에 일을 시작하다
- **apply to a company** — 회사에 지원하다
- **apply for a job** — 일자리에 지원하다
- **apply for a position** — 직무/포지션에 지원하다
- **a company close to home** — 집에서 가까운 회사
- **work as a full-stack developer** — 풀스택 개발자로 일하다
- **work as a front-end developer** — 프론트엔드 개발자로 일하다
- **work at a company** — 회사에서 일하다
- **work for a company** — 회사에 소속되어 일하다
- **work from home** — 재택근무하다
- **work on a project** — 프로젝트를 작업하다

### 퇴사·이직

- **quit my job** — 직장을 그만두다
- **leave the company** — 회사를 그만두다
- **become interested in ~** — ~에 관심이 생기다
- **end up working at ~** — 결국 ~에서 일하게 되다
- **get back to work** — 다시 일을 시작하다
- **send out my resume** — 이력서를 여러 곳에 보내다
- **delay my paycheck** — 내 월급 지급을 미루다
- **collect unemployment benefits** — 실업급여를 받다

### 직장생활

- **have a good work-life balance** — 워라밸이 좋다
- **get along with my coworkers** — 동료들과 잘 지내다
- **have lunch with my former boss** — 전 상사와 점심을 먹다
- **while I was unemployed** — 내가 실업 상태였을 때
- **at my current company** — 현재 회사에서

</details>
<details>
<summary>예문 </summary>

I work 34 hours a week.

I work seven hours a day from Monday to Thursday, and six hours on Fridays.

I started working in 2021, when I was 31.

I applied to a company close to home, and I worked there as a full-stack developer for a year and a half.

Then I became interested in front-end development, so I quit my job and applied for a front-end developer position. I ended up working at my second company for another year and a half.

I left that company because they kept delaying our paychecks.

While I was unemployed and collecting unemployment benefits, I had lunch with my former boss from my first company. He told me, “This is no time to be sitting around. You need to get back to work.”

So I started sending out my resume again, and that's how I ended up at my current company.

My current company has a good work-life balance, and the people are great.

</details>

## Study

<details>
<summary>표현 </summary>

### 공부 시간·장소

- **study after work** — 퇴근 후 공부하다
- **study before work** — 출근 전에 공부하다
- **study at home** — 집에서 공부하다
- **study by myself** — 혼자 공부하다
- **in my free time** — 내 여가 시간에
- **after work** — 퇴근 후에
- **on weekends** — 주말에
- **in the evening** — 저녁에

### 공부가 잘 안 될 때

- **try to study** — 공부하려고 노력하다
- **have trouble ~ing** — ~하는 데 어려움을 겪다
- **can't seem to ~** — 아무리 해도 ~가 잘 안 되다
- **stick to ~** — ~을 꾸준히 하다
- **stick to studying** — 공부를 꾸준히 하다
- **find it hard to ~** — ~하기 어렵다고 느끼다

</details>
<details>
<summary>예문 </summary>

I try to study after work, but it's not going very well.

I have trouble studying after work.

I want to study after work, but I just can't seem to stick to it.
(I try to study at home by myself after work, but I can't seem to stick to it.)

</details>

## Workout

<details>
<summary>표현 </summary>

### 운동 루틴

- **work out every morning** — 매일 아침 운동하다
- **work out almost every morning** — 거의 매일 아침 운동하다
- **work out from Monday to Friday** — 월요일부터 금요일까지 운동하다
- **do CrossFit** — 크로스핏을 하다
- **go to a CrossFit class** — 크로스핏 수업에 가다
- **go to the gym** — 헬스장에 가다

### 수면과 운동

- **go to bed before 1 a.m.** — 새벽 1시 전에 자다
- **stay up past 1 a.m.** — 새벽 1시 넘어서까지 깨어 있다
- **stay up late** — 늦게까지 깨어 있다
- **get up early** — 일찍 일어나다
- **go to the morning class** — 아침 수업에 가다
- **go to the evening class instead** — 대신 저녁 수업에 가다

> **If I go to bed before 1 a.m., I go to the morning class.**
> **If I stay up past 1, I go to the evening class instead.**

### 운동 기간·변화

- **work out for about a year** — 약 1년 동안 운동하다
- **still enjoy ~ing** — 여전히 ~하는 것을 즐기다
- **be in good shape** — 몸 상태가 좋다
- **be in the best shape I've ever been in** — 살면서 몸 상태가 가장 좋다
- **improve my posture** — 자세를 개선하다
- **put on some muscle** — 근육이 좀 붙다
- **get stronger** — 더 강해지다
</details>
<details>
<summary>예문 </summary>
I work out almost every morning from Monday to Friday. I do CrossFit.

If I go to bed before 1 a.m., I go to the 7:30 a.m. class. But if I stay up past 1, I go to the evening class instead.

I've been working out for about a year now, and I'm still enjoying it.

I'm probably in the best shape I've ever been in. My posture has improved, and I've put on some muscle.

I still have a long way to go, but I want to keep working at it and get stronger.

</details>

## Cooking

<details>
<summary>표현 </summary>

### 요리 빈도

- **cook two or three times a week** — 일주일에 두세 번 요리하다
- **cook at home** — 집에서 요리하다
- **make dinner at home** — 집에서 저녁을 만들다
- **every now and then** — 가끔
- **most of the time** — 대부분의 경우

### 요리 실력

- **be a good cook** — 요리를 잘하다
- **be good at cooking** — 요리를 잘하다
- **be great at making ~** — ~을 만드는 데 능숙하다
- **make something decent** — 적당히 괜찮은 걸 만들다
- **make something that's edible** — 먹을 만한 걸 만들다
- **make fancy dishes** — 근사한 요리를 만들다

### 맛 관련

- **the secret to my cooking** — 내 요리의 비결
- **taste a little off** — 맛이 뭔가 이상하다/애매하다
- **add some MSG** — MSG를 좀 넣다
- **add a little more seasoning** — 양념을 조금 더 넣다
- **adjust the seasoning** — 간을 조절하다
- **make it taste better** — 더 맛있게 만들다

> **Something tastes a little off.**
> 뭔가 맛이 좀 이상해.

> **It tastes a little bland.**
> 좀 싱거워.

</details>

<details>
<summary>예문 </summary>
I cook two or three times a week.

I think I'm a pretty good cook, but my younger sibling always tells me, “Please, just stop.”

The secret to my cooking is MSG. If something tastes a little off, I just add some MSG.

I've been living on my own for over ten years, so even though I'm not great at making fancy dishes, I can make something that's decent and perfectly edible.

I don't really enjoy making a bunch of different side dishes. I prefer making things like spicy pork, pork and bean sprout stir-fry, or dak-galbi.

They're all basically one-dish meals, so I can just put everything on one plate and eat in front of my computer.

</details>
