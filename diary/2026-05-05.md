## 5/5 화

**A:** If you could travel abroad, who would you want to go with?

**B:** Honestly, I'd love to go with someone I'm dating — like a boyfriend.

**A:** Really? Why not friends or family?

**B:** I've actually traveled with both. I traveled with my mom once, and we ended up butting heads the whole time. And I've gone on trips with close friends too, but I still felt kind of on edge.

**A:** Huh, interesting.

**B:** And traveling solo just feels lonely, you know? So I've come to realize that who you're with matters way more than where you're going.

**A:** That totally makes sense.

**B:** I actually heard about this group of friends who put a little money aside every month and take a short hotel trip once a year. It sounded fun at first, but when I actually pictured myself doing the same thing, I still felt uneasy.

**A:** Even with close friends?

**B:** Yeah, weirdly enough. But somehow, when I pictured doing it with a boyfriend, it felt completely natural.

**A:** Maybe a partner just feels more like family than a friend does.

**B:** Exactly. With friends, I still feel like I have to keep up appearances — like, I can't really show when I'm tired or in a bad mood. But with family or a partner, I feel like I can just be real.

**A:** Right. You can let your guard down and just be yourself. They say people kind of act like kids around someone they're really comfortable with. Apparently, there's actually a term for it.

**B:** Ha, I looked that up once. Apparently, it's a real thing. I even saw a girl talking in a baby voice on the subway platform once.

**A:** Oof. That's a bit much to watch.

**B:** Right? But honestly, what's even worse is when couples get super touchy in public. I once saw a couple practically all over each other right there on the platform. It made me cringe so hard.

**A:** Oh no. I've seen stuff like that too. Like... get a room.

**B:** It just made me think, "I never want to be that couple."

**A:** Yeah. Read the room, people.

**B:** 하, 나 그거 한번 찾아봤거든. 진짜 실제로 있는 현상이더라. 나도 한번 지하철 승강장에서 아기 목소리로 말하는 여자를 본 적 있어.

## 자주 쓰는 표현 정리 (영한)

1. **butt heads**: 티격태격하다, 의견이 충돌하다

- We ended up **butting heads** the whole trip. / 여행 내내 티격태격했어.
- Those two always **butt heads** at team meetings. / 저 둘은 팀 회의 때마다 항상 충돌해.

2. **on edge**: 긴장한, 불안한, 신경이 곤두선

- I felt kind of **on edge** the whole time. / 내내 좀 긴장되고 불편했어.
- She's been **on edge** ever since the meeting. / 그녀는 회의 이후로 계속 불안해하고 있어.

3. **traveling solo**: 혼자 여행하는 것, 혼자 여행하다

- **Traveling solo** can be liberating, but also lonely. / 혼자 여행하면 자유롭지만 외롭기도 해.
- I've been **traveling solo** for years now. / 나 몇 년째 혼자 여행 다니고 있어.

4. **who you're with matters more than where you're going**: 어디를 가느냐보다 누구와 함께 가느냐가 더 중요하다

- In the end, **who you're with matters way more than where you're going**. / 결국 어디를 가느냐보다 누구와 가느냐가 훨씬 중요해.
- That's why I think **who you're with matters more than where you're going**. / 그래서 나는 어디를 가느냐보다 누구와 가느냐가 더 중요하다고 생각해.

5. **picture yourself ~ing**: 자신이 ~하는 모습을 상상하다

- When I **pictured myself doing it**, I still felt uneasy. / 내가 그걸 하는 모습을 상상해보니까 여전히 불편했어.
- Try to **picture yourself living there** for a year. / 1년 동안 거기서 사는 네 모습을 상상해봐.

6. **keep up appearances**: 겉으로 괜찮은 척하다, 체면을 유지하다

- With friends, I still feel like I have to **keep up appearances**. / 친구들이랑 있으면 아직도 괜찮은 척해야 할 것 같아.
- Even when things are bad at home, they **keep up appearances**. / 집에서 힘든 일이 있어도 겉으로는 괜찮은 척해.

7. **let your guard down**: 경계를 풀다, 긴장을 풀다, 마음을 편하게 열다

- With a partner, you can finally **let your guard down**. / 파트너와 있으면 드디어 긴장을 풀 수 있어.
- It took a while before she **let her guard down** around him. / 그녀가 그 사람 앞에서 마음을 편하게 열기까지 시간이 좀 걸렸어.

8. **act like a little kid**: 어린애처럼 굴다

- They say people **act like little kids** around someone they're comfortable with. / 편한 사람 앞에서는 어린애처럼 굴게 된다고 하더라.
- Stop **acting like a little kid** — you're an adult. / 어린애처럼 굴지 마. 넌 어른이잖아.

9. **talk in a baby voice**: 아기 같은 목소리로 말하다, 애교 섞인 목소리로 말하다

- There was a girl **talking in a baby voice** on the subway platform. / 지하철 승강장에서 아기 같은 목소리로 말하는 여자가 있었어.
- I really can't stand it when people **talk in a baby voice** in public. / 사람들이 공공장소에서 아기 같은 목소리로 말하는 거 진짜 못 견디겠어.

10. **cringe**: 오글거리다, 민망해서 움츠러들다

※ **cringe는 기본적으로 동사**로 쓰고, 형용사로는 보통 **cringey / cringe-worthy**를 사용해.

- It made me **cringe** so hard. / 진짜 너무 오글거렸어.
- Looking back at my old posts makes me **cringe**. / 예전에 올린 글 보면 너무 오글거려.
- That was so **cringey**. / 그거 진짜 오글거렸어.

11. **get a room**: 방 잡아라, 남들 보는 데서 그러지 마라

공공장소에서 지나치게 스킨십하는 커플에게 하는 말. 실제로 방을 잡으라는 뜻이라기보다 **"남들 보는 데서 그러지 말고 어디 가서 해라"**라는 비꼬는 표현.

- They were all over each other in the subway. Like, **get a room**. / 지하철에서 둘이 엄청 붙어있더라. 진짜, 방 잡아라.
- Everyone was staring at them thinking, "**Get a room already.**" / 다들 속으로 "방 잡아라, 진짜"라고 생각했을 거야.

12. **I never want to be that couple**: 나는 절대 저런 커플은 되고 싶지 않다

- It just made me think, "**I never want to be that couple.**" / 그냥 "나는 절대 저런 커플은 되지 말아야지"라는 생각이 들었어.
- Every time I see excessive PDA, I think, "**I never want to be that couple.**" / 과한 공공장소 애정표현을 볼 때마다 "나는 절대 저런 커플은 되지 말아야지" 싶어.

13. **read the room**: 분위기와 상황을 파악하다, 눈치를 보다

- **Read the room, people.** / 눈치 좀 챙겨라, 제발.
- He kept making jokes at the funeral. He just can't **read the room**. / 장례식에서 계속 농담을 하더라. 진짜 눈치가 없어.

14. **a little ~ goes a long way**: 조금만 ~해도 큰 효과가 있다

- **A little self-awareness goes a long way.** / 조금만 자기 객관화가 있어도 큰 도움이 돼.
- **A little kindness goes a long way** in building trust. / 작은 친절만으로도 신뢰를 쌓는 데 큰 도움이 돼.
